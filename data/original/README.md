# Original-source archive

Access date: 2026-08-11.

This folder records original publication material and benchmark specifications.
Source PDFs are not redistributed in this public archive due to publisher copyright restrictions. 
Researchers can access the original publications via the provided DOIs:

1. **Fyffe et al. (1968)**:
   - D. E. Fyffe, W. W. Hines, and N. K. Lee, "System Reliability Allocation and a Computational Algorithm", *IEEE Transactions on Reliability*, vol. R-17, no. 2, pp. 64-69, 1968.
   - DOI: [10.1109/TR.1968.5217522](https://doi.org/10.1109/TR.1968.5217522)
   - Transcribed Table I data is archived verbatim in `fyffe_1968_table_i.csv`.

2. **Garg & Sharma (2013)**:
   - Harish Garg and S. P. Sharma, "Reliability-redundancy allocation problem of pharmaceutical plant", *Journal of Engineering Science and Technology*, vol. 8, no. 2, pp. 190-198, 2013.
   - Available via journal portal: [JESTEC Archive](https://jestec.taylors.edu.my/)
